Standard Operating Procedures

Reference guide for the Munder Skiles finishing workflow.

New service order intake

1
Upload the PO PDF on the PO Intake page

AI reads and extracts all fields automatically.

2
Review extracted fields

Verify project name, PO number, color, and container date. Correct anything missed.

3
Check custom color flag

If flagged, trigger a sample work order in ERP before saving or scheduling.

4
Save to Notion

Pushes a new record to Service Orders with status Received and all date milestones calculated.

5
Download both labels

Catalyst and Paint Base labels auto-fill from the PO data. Open in Word and print on Brother 4"×2" stock.

Custom color sample workflow

1
Flag triggers automatically

If the PO mentions a custom or non-standard color, the warning appears on intake.

2
Trigger shop work order in ERP

Do this before saving to Notion. Order status will auto-set to Work Order Created.

3
Send sample to Munder Skiles HQ

Update Notion status to Sample Sent.

4
Await approval

Once HQ approves, update to Sample Approved. Only then schedule into production.

Status definitions

Received PO logged, no action taken yet
Work Order Created Internal WO issued to shop (custom color orders)
Sample Sent Custom color sample shipped to HQ
Sample Approved HQ has signed off on color
Scheduled On the production schedule in ERP
In Production Active on the shop floor
Shipped Completed and returned to Munder Skiles
